## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The system is internally coherent and follows DESIGN.md's letter with rare discipline — flat surfaces, hairline borders, shadow-state machine, drag ghost, glass FAB, Inter-only, bilingual constants. But the product voice ("The Mission Log", "events are facts") is barely audible: swap the copy and this is an interchangeable kanban-analytics-dashboard kit. Nothing in the interaction language is native to a job search whose history is append-only. The single biggest specificity problem is semantic drift: the closed status set — the domain model made visual — is contradicted across views. Board pills say Applied=yellow, Interview=purple; the activity chart renders Applied as indigo `#6366f1` (a hue nowhere in DESIGN.md); the funnel renders every status including Rejected in Signal Blue; round "scheduled" is blue on cards but purple in the timeline; filter chips borrow status hues for date-range meanings. The analytics view looks designed by a second, unrelated hand — precisely the view that must be the truth-teller.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI detector found 10 advisory/warning findings (5 in src/: undocumented `#475569`, shadow alpha, Google brand `#4285f4`, two 9px type sizes in JobCard; 5 in index.html: overused-font Inter ×2, `#cbd5e1`, 3px radius, `#475569` — most are false positives from the Tailwind-CDN token config and brand color). In-page scan of 5 routes: the board is heavily noisy (696 findings: 188 low-contrast, 187 nested-cards, 184 tiny-text, 126 undersized-ui-text) while timeline/schedule/stats are light (4/7/9). The detector caught what the LLM review missed: **10px functional text on status chips** ("Recherche", "Zu bewerben" — not uppercase micro-tags, so the 14px floor is actually violated), **white-on-status pill contrast at 2.5:1** (white on emerald/teal/rose), **`#9ca3af` at 2.5:1 on the login page**, and **purple/indigo/violet headings on stats** (`ai-color-palette` — independently confirming the hue drift). False positives: shadow alpha, Google brand blue, Tailwind config tokens, Inter overused-font heuristic.

## Overall Impression

A calm, disciplined execution of a system that knows its rules — and breaks its own vocabulary exactly where truth-telling matters most: the analytics view. The interaction mechanics (optimistic moves, undo, drag states, focus traps) are genuinely production-grade; the voice, the color semantics, and the expert path are not. Single biggest opportunity: make the closed status set hold everywhere — then the "facts on record" promise becomes visible.

## What's Working

1. **Depth-means-state is executed with rare discipline.** Rest=shadow-sm, hover=shadow-md, drag=rotate-3 ghost+ring, drop=dashed primary silhouette, overlay=shadow-xl+blur, FAB=the one glass object. The elevation vocabulary isn't just written, it ships — this is the app's calm, mechanical reliability.
2. **Recovery design is genuinely good.** Optimistic updates with 6s Undo toast, Escape everywhere, focus traps in both modals, drag auto-scroll with edge fades, touch long-press drag with vibration, `prefers-reduced-motion` support, `@media (pointer: fine)` so card actions never hover-lock on touch.
3. **Bilingual/dark/theme discipline is production-grade.** Every component has dark variants, nearly all copy is constants-driven EN+DE with German default, language and theme reachable from both desktop and mobile.

## Priority Issues

**P0 — Status-hue semantics contradict the closed set across views.**
- **What:** Dashboard series use indigo `#6366f1` (Dashboard.tsx:358); the funnel renders every status incl. Rejected in Signal Blue (Dashboard.tsx:221); rejection-depth uses a slate/indigo/purple/red ramp (397–400); filter chips borrow green/yellow status hues for date ranges (JobBoard.tsx:1103, 1111); round "scheduled" = blue on cards (JobCard.tsx:241) but purple in the timeline (TimelineView.tsx:179). Detector independently flags purple/violet/indigo headings on stats (`ai-color-palette` ×4).
- **Why it matters:** The closed status set is the classification language; every contradiction forces re-learning and undercuts trust in the analytics view — the view that must be the truth-teller.
- **Fix:** Map chart series to the closed set (Applied=amber #fbbf24, Interview=purple, Rejected=red, Added=neutral/primary), delete `#6366f1`, tint funnel bars by status, unify round-status hues in one shared constant.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable colorize` (then `$impeccable distill` on the funnel)

**P0 — German copy integrity breaks the "bilingual is mandatory" commitment.**
- **What:** Hardcoded English `"${position} at ${company}"` in German timeline/recent activity (TimelineView.tsx:30/49/63, Dashboard.tsx:163); `toLowerCase()` producing "200 alle ereignisse" (TimelineView.tsx:233); "Neue Chance hinzufügen" vs "Job hinzufügen" vs "Bewerbung" in delete body; "Ihre Zielrollen" vs "du"; hardcoded "LANGUAGE"/"Drop Here"; `<html lang="en">` (index.html:2) with German content; title "CareerTrack Scheduler".
- **Why it matters:** German is the default language; these are visible daily and erode the personal-tool trust.
- **Fix:** Parametrized strings in constants ("…bei…"), `lang` bound to app language, proper capitalization, term audit against CONTEXT.md.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

**P1 — AI coach failure is styled as success, and the daily ritual is scattered.**
- **What:** `generateTaskAdvice` returns an error string rendered inside the success-styled "Coach Ratschlag" panel (geminiService.ts:55; ScheduleView.tsx:124–133); no retry; advice not persisted (refresh erases the day's coaching); every non-Break block shows its own filled-blue "Fokus starten" (ScheduleView.tsx:146–161) — four identical primaries at once.
- **Why it matters:** The flagship AI feature's failure state lies about itself — the worst trust break in a daily ritual; four simultaneous identical primaries dilute the One Signal Rule into button noise.
- **Fix:** Distinct danger-tinted error surface with role="alert" + "Erneut versuchen", one consistent label, persist advice per block; collapse to one primary "Fokus starten" entry (per-day) with secondary per-block buttons.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

**P1 — High-stakes delete is under-emphasized and unacknowledged.**
- **What:** Cascade warning is plain gray 14px text with no icon (DeleteConfirmModal.tsx:61–65); title says "Job löschen?" while body says "Bewerbung"; after confirm: silence — no toast, no undo.
- **Why it matters:** Deleting an application deletes its interview history — irreversible loss in a real search; the moment deserves weight and post-action acknowledgment.
- **Fix:** Danger-tinted callout with icon ("Interview-Runden (n) werden mitgelöscht"), post-delete toast (with Undo where feasible).
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`

**P1 — Accessibility: quantified contrast failures, 10px functional text, no keyboard path for status.**
- **What:** Detector: white-on-emerald/teal pills 2.5:1, `#9ca3af` 2.5:1 (login placeholder), `#6b7280` on `#f6f6f8` 4.48:1 (just under AA) across every page; 10px functional text on status chips (not uppercase micro-tags); A-side: unlabeled hamburger/logout/modal-X (Header.tsx:44, Sidebar.tsx:97, JobModal.tsx:209), no focus-visible styling on sidebar nav, HTML5 DnD has no keyboard path (only the 32px chevron), dark round-status date ~3:1.
- **Why it matters:** Sam can't complete core flows keyboard-only and misses labels; 2.5:1 text fails WCAG AA at the most common colors.
- **Fix:** Tinted pill text at 600/700 family levels (not white-on-500), raise gray-400 usage to 500, 11px floor for all functional text, aria-labels + focus-visible rings, keyboard-operable status change.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable audit` (a11y pass) + `$impeccable adapt`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user):** No keyboard shortcuts at all — not even `/` for search or `N` for new job; no bulk actions (184 applications advance one at a time); add flow re-defaults to RESEARCH with a 7-option status select every time; no duplicate-last-entry; filters/sort/accordion are component-local and reset on every navigation.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent):** Mobile hamburger, logout icon button, and modal close X are unlabeled (title- or icon-only); board status changes have no keyboard path (HTML5 DnD is not keyboard-operable; the 32px chevron is the only alternative); active language-switcher segment and pills carry no `aria-pressed`; `<html lang="en">` makes German content mispronounced by screen readers; white-on-emerald/teal pills at 2.5:1 and dark-mode date text ~3:1 fail AA.

**Casey (distracted mobile user):** Card action icons ~24px and next-status chevron ~32px — below thumb-target size; quick-nav chips ~34px tall; navigating between routes silently resets filters, sort, and the mobile accordion — returning from an interruption means re-configuring the board; mobile shows one status column at a time, other five remembered only from the chip bar. Wins to preserve: FAB is a proper large target with aria-label, toast sits above it, long-press drag with vibration.

- Timeline `job_added` blue and `job_applied` indigo are 4px-adjacent blues, nearly indistinguishable at 12px.

## Questions to Consider

1. If events are facts, why is the page's centerpiece — the "Bewerbungstrichter" — a snapshot of *current* state rendered in one uniform blue? Which truth is this dashboard telling?
2. The AI coach asks a single time-block "what should I do right now" — would one "plan my day" entry point (one CTA, one persisted answer) better serve the daily ritual than four per-block buttons?
3. The board hides interview rounds below `2xl` and buries the dossier in modals — is the card a summary or the record? If the log is the product, should the record be reachable without a detour?
4. For a single user with a long-lived search, why is every filter/sort/accordion preference ephemeral — should "how I like to see my log" be saved once?
